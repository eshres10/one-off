## 1. What One Off does

One Off runs the marketplace where your pieces are listed, takes payment from buyers and pays
you, less commission and card processing, after each order is delivered.

## 2. Commission

Founding makers pay the founding rate for their founding period, then the standard rate, worked
out on each piece's price when it sells (never on shipping). The rate on an order never changes
after the sale.

## 3. Payouts

Money for an order is released a few days after it's delivered and paid out to your bank weekly
through Stripe.

## 4. Making and shipping

You make, pack and ship your pieces by the ship-by date and add tracking.

## 5. Returns

There are no change-of-mind returns. If a piece is faulty or not as described, you'll repair,
replace or refund it. Australian Consumer Law applies.

## 6. Photos and content

Your photos stay yours. You give One Off permission to show them on One Off and to promote your
pieces.

## 7. Leaving

You can close your shop at any time. Open orders still need to be fulfilled.
