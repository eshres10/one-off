## Faulty or not as described

> Your rights under Australian Consumer Law, and how we resolve problems.

If your piece arrives faulty, damaged, or isn't as described, tell us within 7 days of delivery with photos, using ‘Report a problem’ on your order. The maker replies within 2 business days and will repair, replace or refund it. For custom pieces made to your measurements, the maker will first alter it to fit what was agreed. If you and the maker can't agree, One Off decides. This applies to every piece, whatever kind it is.

This doesn't affect your rights under Australian Consumer Law, which continue after the 7 days.

## Change-of-mind returns

> The 14-day window for ready-to-ship pieces, the condition required, return postage, and why made-to-order, custom and one-of-one pieces are excluded.

Ready-to-ship pieces can be returned for a change of mind within 14 days of delivery, unworn, with tags and original packaging. Start the return from your order, send it back to the maker with tracking within 7 days, and you're refunded the item price once they receive it. Return postage is paid by you, and original shipping isn't refunded.

Made-to-order and custom pieces are made after you buy, and one-of-ones are the only one there is, so they can't be returned for a change of mind. Please check the size guide or ask the maker before ordering.
