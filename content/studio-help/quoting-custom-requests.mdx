## How a request reaches you

A buyer describes what they want in **Have it made**: the kind of piece, the occasion and date,
photos and words, fabric and colour, their measurements, budget and where they are. We show
them the makers who fit, and they pick up to three to send it to. If you're one of them, it
lands in **Studio → Custom requests** and you have 48 hours to quote or pass.

## Your quote

- **One price, fabric included.** The panel shows whether it sits within their budget and
  what you'd get after commission and card fees.
- **Ready by:** pick a date you can keep. They see it on your quote.
- **Fittings:** only the options you offer in your custom work settings are shown.
- **A note to them:** it's shown big, so say how you'd make it. A sketch helps.
- **Measurements:** tick that you've checked them. You can't send without it.

You can edit a quote until the buyer accepts it; they see it marked as updated. If it's not for
you, pass with a reason. The buyer only sees "Not available this time".

## After they accept

They pay in full and Off One holds it. Mark each step from the order: sketch shared, fabric
bought, fitting, made, sent. **Fabric bought** pays you 30% of the piece price straight away;
the rest follows 3 days after delivery, like any order. Custom pieces can't be returned for a
change of mind; faulty or not as agreed goes through Report a problem.
