## Before you quote

- Ask for the measurements you need and the date they need it by.
- Confirm the fabric and colour, and send a swatch photo if you can.

## Your quote

- One total price, the deposit, and when you'll start and finish.
- Say what's included (fittings, changes) and what isn't.

## After they accept

The deposit is paid through checkout, and the agreed details are saved with the order so you
both have the same record.
