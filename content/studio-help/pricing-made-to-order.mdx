## Start with your costs

- **Fabric and trims** for one piece, including what you lose when cutting.
- **Your time**, at an hourly rate you'd be happy to work for.
- **Packaging** and anything else that goes in the box.

## Then check your take-home

The listing editor shows what you get as you type the price: the price, less Off One's
commission and card processing. Shipping is yours in full.

## Making time

Choose the making time you can keep even in a busy week. Buyers see it before they buy, and
you can [set your weekly capacity](/studio/settings#made-to-order) so you never take on too much.
