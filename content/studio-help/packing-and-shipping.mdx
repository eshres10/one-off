## Packing

- Fold with tissue, and put the piece in a sealed bag inside the mailer in case it gets wet.
- Include the packing slip from the order (Studio → Orders → Packing slip).
- A handwritten note goes a long way.

## Posting

- Ready-to-ship pieces go out within your dispatch time (2 business days by default).
- Made-to-order pieces go out by the date shown on the order.

## Tracking

Add the tracking number in Studio and tap **Mark shipped**; we email the buyer. You can also
reply to the new-order email with the tracking number.
