## What to shoot

1. The front, on a person if you can, otherwise on a hanger against a plain wall.
2. The back, and anything special about it (an open back, buttons, a tie).
3. A close-up of the fabric so buyers can see the weave and the sheen.

## Light

- Shoot near a window in daylight, with the sun behind you rather than behind the piece.
- Avoid overhead lights; they turn ivory yellow and black grey.
- Your phone's camera is enough. Turn off filters.

## Before you upload

- Steam the piece so creases don't read as faults.
- Use a portrait (4:5) crop; that's how pieces show in Discover.
